Hello!

I am Molly Hartman, a film and video editor from the New Jersey Pine Barrens. I am currently in my senior year at Savannah College of Art and Design and am pursuing a Bachelors of Fine Arts in Film and Television.

Ever since I was young, I've been making videos. Whether it be filming my baby brother with my family camcorder, making skits in my bedroom with my iPad mini, or helping film student projects with a Red Komodo, I've been creating films. My passion is helping stories come to life through creative editing techniques.

I am also a hobbyist illustrator and web designer. I've been drawing for as long as I can remember and have been drawing digitally for about 10 years now. Before I decided to go to school for film, I was actually going to go for animation! I have been coding in HTML and CSS since 2023 as a side hobby, and am currently learning JavaScript. This website was built using both.

You can see my personal website here and find some examples of my art here.
